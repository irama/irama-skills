# Collaboration-move codebook v2

Each record shows the tail of what the assistant produced and what the human replied. Assign
**one** move and **one** outcome.

## Moves that shape the work

- **BRIEF** — new direction, requirements or scope. Nothing corrected. Opens a new front.
- **REFOCUS** — the assistant answered a smaller question than the one that mattered,
  competently. The human names the question the work should have been answering. The output is
  usually *correct*; that is what makes this hard to spot.
- **LOGIC** — a list, taxonomy, label pair, template, fork or menu whose members are not the
  same kind of thing, do not cover the ground, or overlap. The human probes the shape of the
  set, not its contents.
- **TASTE** — the output is not wrong and answers the right question. The human wants it
  **different**: better named, better worded, consolidated, simpler, more in their voice.
  Not a correction. A judgement only the owner can make.

## Moves that check the work

- **TEST** — the assistant declared something done or verified on evidence it produced itself.
  The human opens the actual artefact and reports what they see.
- **REMIND** — the assistant asserted what a file, skill or standard does, from memory or its
  own earlier summary, then reasoned on top of it. The human asks whether that is actually true.

## Moves that unblock the work

- **HELP** — the assistant hit a boundary it **cannot cross**: a login wall, a key it cannot
  mint, an installed app, a fact about the human's own life. The human supplies it. The
  defining feature is that the assistant *could not have done this*.

## Moves about the collaboration itself

- **APPROVE** — the assistant proposed next steps and the human **chooses among them**. A
  decision about what to do next, distinct from agreeing that finished work is fine.
- **EMBED** — something worth keeping is sitting in chat. The human asks for it to be written
  where it will be found again.
- **META** — managing the communication or the process, not the work. Restate that more
  clearly, what does this term mean, this is too long, run these in parallel or series.

## Not a move

- **ACCEPT** — agrees finished work is fine. Nothing corrected, chosen or directed.
- **ADMIN** — logistics with no bearing on work or collaboration.
- **UNCLEAR** — cannot tell.

## Outcome

- **D** the work changed what it was aiming at · **R** rebuilt or substantially reworked ·
- **P** a detail was corrected · **N** no change, or the record does not show one.

## Rules

- One move per record. If two apply, pick the one that did the most work.
- **APPROVE will be the largest category. Code it honestly.**
- **TASTE is not a failure.** Ask whether the original was wrong, or merely not what they wanted.
- Do not reward length. A four-word reply can be REFOCUS; a long one can be BRIEF.
- Never skip a record.
